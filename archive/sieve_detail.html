Palindromic Symmetry of RRS

Every RRS has reflective symmetry around the midpoint Pn# / 2. If x is an anti-node, then Pn# - x is also an anti-node.

Nested Sub-Cycles & Rotational Deletion

Adding a prime pn expands the period width to Pn# = pn × Pn-1#. We see pn copies of the sub-cycle. Because they are coprime, the multiples of pn rotate and delete exactly one anti-node from each sub-cycle.
